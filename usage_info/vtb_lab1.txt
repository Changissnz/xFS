There are two important attributes in Vector Tracking Bot that require explicit mention. 
The first is the PRNGs of the tracking group are LCGs derived from one PRNG user input. 
The second is the target PRNG is used, as a form of noise induction, alongside each 
tracker agent's PRNG to influence the tracking agent's prediction of the target's next 
location. See method<MVTrackingGroupTypeSO.move_MVAgents> for details on this.
