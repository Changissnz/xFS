The difficulty of Introspection Bot rests on the stochastic variance introduced by the 
introspection process, and this process revolves around either a breadth-first or depth-first 
search process. For reactive introspection, a change in PRNGs results in changes to the node/edge 
deltas of addition and subtraction during the traversal process. For variable memory ("varmem") 
introspection, a change in PRNGs affects the node priority function, responsible for ranking 
neighbors in order of travel from current node to next. 

There are five trials in this lab to demonstrate all properties of Introspection Bot are working 
properly. The first three are on a reactive introspection process, the last two on variable memory.
